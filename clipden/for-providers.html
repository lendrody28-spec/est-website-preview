What your profile can hold

Enough context for a confident hello.

A provider profile can include your website, pictures, calendar state, qualifications, bio, contact choices, locations, services, and reviews.

Bio + qualifications

Tell people how you work. A licensed badge note can explain whether status is self-attested or pending verify.

Calendar control

Keep your calendar private until you manually set availability. No assumed hours, no surprise bookings.

Contact first

Clients can send a profile and style details so you can reply with the right next step.

Manual availability rule: the “Book a time” button and bookable slots appear only after you manually publish availability. Before that, the clear action is “Contact provider”.
