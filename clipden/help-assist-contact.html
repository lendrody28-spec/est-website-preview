Clear help, without pretending the stub is live.

Soft Chair honesty means we say what this is: a free, static preparation preview. There is no account, inbox, payment, live booking engine, or human support queue behind these buttons yet.

Help with the idea

Read the quick answers below, or use the contact form to draft the kind of message a future support path could receive.

Can I book a haircut here?

No. This site does not create bookings. In the planned product, a booking button appears only after a provider manually sets availability.

Is the map live?

No. The map is a visual placeholder. The direction is to use a free map stack, with no paid map keys or hosting spend.

What does Assist mean?

Assist is the public help language for guidance and complaints. We minimise technical labels and do not present this preview as an automated support service.

Are licence badges official?

No. “Licensed” or “Pending verify” needs a clear status note. This preview does not perform official verification.
