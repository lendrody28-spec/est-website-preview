Simple. Smart. Secure.

Drive → Detect → Assess → Assist → Notify. Clear labels; Assist means human help.

01

02

03

Relevant information is processed — no always-on surveillance claims.

04

Available now Smart Guardian Crash Assist / Emergency Help can initiate appropriate assistance. Assist means human help — not a chatbot.
